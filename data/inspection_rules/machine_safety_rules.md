# Machine Safety Rules

## Rule ID: MACH-001
Category: Machine Guarding
Inspection Area: Operating Machinery
Requirement: Machine guards must be installed and closed during operation to prevent contact with moving parts.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Stop the machine and restore the guard before restarting operation.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: MACH-002
Category: Emergency Stop Access
Inspection Area: Machine Control Area
Requirement: Emergency stop buttons must be clearly visible, accessible, and free from obstruction.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Remove obstruction immediately and verify emergency stop accessibility.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: MACH-003
Category: Exposed Moving Parts
Inspection Area: Rotating or Moving Equipment
Requirement: Belts, pulleys, gears, chains, and rotating shafts must not be exposed during operation.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Stop equipment and install suitable guarding before use.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: MACH-004
Category: Lockout Tagout
Inspection Area: Maintenance Area
Requirement: Machines under maintenance must be isolated from power sources and marked with lockout-tagout indicators.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Stop maintenance activity until lockout-tagout procedure is verified.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: MACH-005
Category: Unsafe Worker Distance
Inspection Area: Active Machine Zone
Requirement: Workers must maintain safe distance from moving machinery unless authorized and protected by safety controls.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Move worker away from unsafe zone and verify safe working distance.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: MACH-006
Category: Electrical Safety
Inspection Area: Machine Electrical Panel
Requirement: Electrical panels must remain closed and must not show exposed wires, damaged insulation, or loose cables.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Stop operation and request electrical maintenance inspection.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: MACH-007
Category: Oil or Fluid Leakage
Inspection Area: Machine Base or Floor Area
Requirement: Oil, coolant, or hydraulic fluid leakage must be cleaned and investigated to avoid slip hazards or machine failure.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Clean the area, mark the hazard, and schedule maintenance inspection.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: MACH-008
Category: Machine Warning Labels
Inspection Area: Machine Surface and Control Panel
Requirement: Warning labels, hazard signs, and machine identification labels must be visible and readable.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Replace missing or unreadable warning labels.
Human Review Required: No
