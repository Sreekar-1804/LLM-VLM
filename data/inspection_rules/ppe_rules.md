# PPE Safety Rules

## Rule ID: PPE-001
Category: Head Protection
Inspection Area: Active Machine Zone
Requirement: Workers must wear safety helmets when working near operating machinery, moving equipment, or overhead hazard areas.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Stop work temporarily and ensure the worker wears an approved safety helmet before resuming operations.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: PPE-002
Category: High-Visibility Clothing
Inspection Area: Production Floor
Requirement: Workers must wear high-visibility safety vests in production zones, logistics areas, and vehicle movement areas.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Notify the worker and supervisor. Correct the PPE violation before continuing work in the area.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: PPE-003
Category: Hand Protection
Inspection Area: Manual Handling Zone
Requirement: Protective gloves must be worn when handling sharp, hot, oily, or rough materials.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Ask the worker to wear suitable gloves before handling the material.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: PPE-004
Category: Eye Protection
Inspection Area: Cutting, Welding, Grinding, or Chemical Handling Area
Requirement: Safety goggles or face shields must be worn in areas with flying particles, sparks, chemicals, or splash risks.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Stop the task until proper eye protection is used.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: PPE-005
Category: Foot Protection
Inspection Area: Warehouse, Workshop, and Production Floor
Requirement: Safety shoes must be worn in areas where heavy objects, sharp objects, or moving equipment are present.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Restrict access to the work area until safety footwear is worn.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: PPE-006
Category: Respiratory Protection
Inspection Area: Dust, Paint, Chemical, or Fume Exposure Area
Requirement: Workers must wear approved respiratory protection when exposed to dust, fumes, paint spray, or chemical vapors.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Stop work and ensure correct respiratory protection is used before continuing.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: PPE-007
Category: Hearing Protection
Inspection Area: High Noise Zone
Requirement: Ear plugs or ear muffs must be worn in areas marked as high-noise zones.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Notify the worker and ensure hearing protection is worn before entering the zone.
Human Review Required: No

## Rule ID: PPE-008
Category: General PPE Compliance
Inspection Area: Restricted Industrial Area
Requirement: Workers entering restricted industrial areas must follow the minimum PPE requirement defined for the zone.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Verify the area-specific PPE checklist and correct any missing PPE before allowing continued access.
Human Review Required: Yes
