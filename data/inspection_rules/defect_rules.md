# Quality Defect Rules

## Rule ID: DEF-001
Category: Surface Crack
Inspection Area: Metal, Plastic, or Composite Component
Requirement: Components must not show visible cracks, fractures, or structural breaks.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Reject the component and send it for detailed quality inspection.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: DEF-002
Category: Surface Scratch
Inspection Area: Painted or Finished Surface
Requirement: Finished components must not show deep scratches, coating damage, or visible surface marks beyond tolerance.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Mark the component for rework or quality review depending on defect size.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: DEF-003
Category: Missing Component
Inspection Area: Assembly Line
Requirement: Assembled products must include all required parts, screws, connectors, labels, and covers.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Stop release of the product and inspect the assembly process.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: DEF-004
Category: Misalignment
Inspection Area: Assembled Product or Machine Part
Requirement: Components must be correctly aligned according to design and assembly requirements.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Send the item for adjustment or rework.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: DEF-005
Category: Contamination
Inspection Area: Product Surface or Packaging Area
Requirement: Components must be free from oil stains, dust, dirt, metal chips, and foreign particles.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Clean the part and inspect whether contamination affected product quality.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: DEF-006
Category: Deformation
Inspection Area: Structural Component
Requirement: Components must not show bending, denting, warping, or other visible shape deformation.
Severity: High
Recommended Action: Reject the component and start defect investigation.
Human Review Required: Yes

## Rule ID: DEF-007
Category: Barcode or Label Issue
Inspection Area: Product Labeling Area
Requirement: Barcode, serial number, and product identification labels must be present, readable, and correctly placed.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Reprint or replace the label before product release.
Human Review Required: No

## Rule ID: DEF-008
Category: Packaging Damage
Inspection Area: Final Packaging Area
Requirement: Packaging must not show tears, crushing, water damage, or incorrect sealing.
Severity: Medium
Recommended Action: Replace damaged packaging and inspect the product inside.
Human Review Required: No
